Notation reference
  • Key Signature: 3/4 sets the time signature
  • Treble Clef1: / Bass Clef1: start a staff (Clef2, Clef3… add more pairs)
  • Line/space number: 0 middle line, positive = up, negative = down
  • Duration: w h q e s — add r for a rest, e.g. qr
  • Accidental prefix: sh- sharp, fl- flat
  • Finger: (1) … (5) after the note
  • Dot (notes): . right after the duration (e.g. q.) adds 50% to its length
  • Dot (rests): . right after the r (e.g. qr., hr.) adds 50% to its length
  • tie between two identical notes joins them — even across a page break into the next clef pair
  • { } groups notes played as a chord
